# Selection research

Checked 15 October 2026. Family: manufacturing sales agreements. This desk tracks time-phased customer commitments and their actual quantities. The existing manufacturing build covers production jobs; rebates cover channel payouts; sales planning covers territory quotas. None is this agreement schedule desk.

US$82,500 per year is an illustrative 25-seat calculation, not a customer invoice. [Salesforce's own pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/manufacturing/cloud/pricing/) lists Sales Core at US$275 per user per month, billed annually. US$275 x 12 x 25 = US$82,500. The public comparison page uses only the sourced per-user price and its annual equivalent, not this assumed team size. Local quotes, taxes, implementation and discounts differ.

Scores out of five: bill 4, data-shaped 5, buyer fit 3, search demand 2, one-session buildable 4, import 5. Total 23/30. Buyer fit is inferred from manufacturers in the target market, not a confirmed customer installation. No live CRM or customer brain access was used and no customer invoice was found in repository context. Search demand is qualitative and no search-volume figure is claimed.

Optimizely content operations scored 1/4/3/2/4/3 = 17, but there was no verified public five-figure price. [Its product page](https://www.optimizely.com/products/content-marketing) supplies the product scope only. Celoxis scored 3/4/3/3/4/4 = 21 based on its [published plans](https://www.celoxis.com/pricing), but project portfolio is already built. Neither is named as a rival to Manufacturing Cloud.

The official [sales-agreement model](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/data-models/guide/sales-agreements.html) links accounts, products, agreements, product lines and schedules. The [sales agreements lesson](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/sales-agreements-foundations/get-started-with-sales-agreements) describes repeat supply commitments. [Data portability documentation](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=xcloud.data_export_portability_across_salesforce.htm&language=en_US&type=5) identifies Data Loader as an export route. The import guide separates supported fields from retained evidence and unimplemented features.

The ten questions in README are demonstrated capabilities. They are not claims that Salesforce cannot answer the same questions with configuration. This build replaces a bounded operational desk, not every feature of Manufacturing Cloud.
